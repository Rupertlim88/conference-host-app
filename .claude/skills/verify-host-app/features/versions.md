# Our versions

A gold demo bar sits at the bottom of every attendee page. It names the version shown, AMS's meeting app (the base) or Our improvements, and its button switches to the other; pressing Control on its own does the same. In Our improvements, each change shows in a gold outline with a label saying what changed. An outline shows even where the matching API gave nothing for that place: then it is empty. Four of the five changes are picked for the demo's fixed abstract, each from its own kind of pool in one matches search per page: Conferences and Symposia ranked for it (each other conference's pool), each calendar day's best talks (the day's pool), each calendar time block's recommended sessions (the block's pool), and a session's talk to come for (the session's pool). The bar also links the organiser and developer consoles.

## Sub-features

- `bar` names the version and switches it; `control` switches on Control pressed and released alone.
- `gold` outlines each change with a label, such as "Our change: related talks".
- `conference-picks` puts first, in a gold box at the top of Conferences and Symposia, the other conferences the matching API lists a pool for around the fixed abstract, each with the first talk its pool lists, the one whose talk scores higher first. Each shows that talk's number and title, kind of match, day and time, and reason. With none, the box is empty. Below the box, AMS's order follows, with the attendee's own conferences marked "Your conference".
- `day-picks` opens each Meeting Calendar day with a gold outline holding the first three talks the matching API lists in that day's pool, one from each session. A day with none shows the outline empty.
- `calendar-picks` opens every time block with a gold outline headed by the block's start time, holding the sessions of up to two talks the matching API lists in that block's pool, or nothing. Each reads its kind of match and `Related talk at <time>`.
- `session-picks` outlines in gold, in its place, the first of the session's talks that the matching API lists in that session's pool: "come for this talk", or "visit this board first" in a poster session. A session with no pool opens its talks with an empty gold box with the same label.
- `api-down`: without the conference API, every outline is empty, apart from one line in the conferences box, one in the day's outline and one note above the calendar's blocks. Session pages show their empty box with no line. AMS's order and sessions stay.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- On any attendee page, the bar at the bottom right. Then open Conferences and Symposia, the Meeting Calendar, or a session.

## Driving it with look.mts

Preconditions:

- The baseline preconditions in the index hold.
- The default fixed abstract is `498388`, 10.1 "Emerging Satellite Constraints on Fire Combustion…", in the 29th Conference on Atmospheric Chemistry. It has three local matches. `498631` (S282) is in the 26th Annual Student Conference, in poster session `75479` (Radar Meteorology), Sunday, January 10, 2027 at 2:30 PM. `498733` (8.1, 4:30 PM) and `498736` (8.2, 5:00 PM) are both in session `76751`, Tuesday, January 12, 2027, in the 19th Symposium on Aerosol-Cloud-Climate Interactions. ## Gotchas

- A look's switch lasts only for that look: the saved state is the one from signing in, so every look starts in the base.
- The bar is fixed to the window, so a full-page screenshot shows it over the page's middle.
- Local matches are placeholders that all score 0.5. The API breaks ties within a pool by talk id. Ties keep AMS's order in the conferences list, and the API's order everywhere else.
- The sample keeps few talks per session; most sessions holding a match show only that talk. `77136` shows two.
- The fixed abstract stays in the database through a stop and launch. Fix it back to `498388` when you finish.
- The `.aria.txt` cannot show whether an outline is empty: an empty box's label is followed by whatever comes next on the page, and two empty boxes in a row put both labels on one `text:` line, such as `Our change: your best talks today Our change: recommended around your abstract`. Look at the `.png` to see what each outline holds.
